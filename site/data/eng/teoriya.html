Англійська: граматика і анотація до заліку

Формат і дата заліку невідомі - спитати на парі. Тут те, що вже давали на парах: Present Simple і Continuous (пара 25.09), verbs + to / -ing (роздатка 06.11), неправильні дієслова (ДЗ 16.10), анотація (нагадування 05.11).

Тема 1. Present Simple і Present Continuous

Present Simple - те, що буває завжди, регулярно, за розкладом, або просто правда.

I / you / we / they work
he / she / it works         (-s!)
Do you work?  Does he work?
I don't work. He doesn't work.

Підказки: always, usually, often, never, every day, on Fridays.

  • I go to the university by tram every day.
  • Water boils at 100 °C.
  • The lecture starts at 8:30. (розклад, навіть про завтра)

Present Continuous - просто зараз, тимчасово (у цей період) або домовлене на майбутнє.

am / is / are + дієслово-ing
I am reading.  He is reading.
Are you reading?  I'm not reading.

Підказки: now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!, this week, this semester, tomorrow (план).

  • Look! It is snowing.
  • This semester we are studying mechanics.
  • I am meeting my friend tomorrow at 5. (домовились)

Не бувають у Continuous (стан, а не дія): know, like, love, want, need, understand, believe, have (мати), see (розуміти), hear, smell (пахнути), think (вважати). Але: I think it's easy (вважаю) - Simple; I'm thinking about it (обмірковую) - Continuous.

Типові помилки: ~~He work~~ → He works; ~~Where you live?~~ → Where do you live?; ~~Does he works?~~ → Does he work?; ~~I am knowing~~ → I know; ~~Now I sit~~ → Now I am sitting.

Тема 2. Verbs + to / -ing

Коли після дієслова йде ще одне дієслово, друге буває з -ing або з to. Залежить від першого. Списки з роздатки:

+ -ing:   admit, avoid, dislike, enjoy,
          fancy, feel like, finish, forget,
          hate, imagine, look forward to,
          mention, mind, miss, practise,
          put off, suggest

+ to:     afford, agree, appear, attempt,
          begin, decide, demand, expect,
          fail, hope, intend, learn, manage,
          offer, plan, pretend, promise,
          refuse, seem, want, would like

обидва:   begin, continue, forget, hate,
          like, love, prefer, remember,
          start
  • Everyone enjoys listening to music.
  • We expected to win the game. I can't afford to buy a bike.
  • I love playing tennis. = I love to play tennis.

Пастки: - I look forward to seeing you - тут to прийменник, тому -ing; - He suggested going (не suggested to go); - I would like to go (не would like going); - підказка: у списку «to» багато дієслів про намір і майбутнє (hope, plan, decide, promise, want).

Тема 3. Неправильні дієслова: де яка форма

V1  після do / does / did, will, can, to
    Did you go?  I didn't see.
V2  Past Simple (yesterday, ago, last ...)
    I went there yesterday.
V3  після have / has; у пасиві після be
    I have seen it.
    It was written in 1952.

Типові помилки: ~~Did you went?~~ → Did you go?; ~~I have saw~~ → I have seen; ~~teached, thinked, buyed~~ → taught, thought, bought; ~~I have lost it yesterday~~ → I lost it yesterday.

Слова-пастки: - found - founded - founded = засновувати; find - found - found = знаходити. «Заснований у 1472» = was founded in 1472. - take an exam = складати (йти на іспит); pass an exam = скласти; fail an exam = провалити.

Повна таблиця дієслів - у вкладці «Словник».

Тема 4. Анотація (annotation) до статті

Анотація - коротко про що текст, 6-10 речень, не переказ усього підряд.

1. Вихідні дані   назва, автор, де і коли
2. Тема           про що стаття одним реченням
3. Зміст          2-4 головні думки
4. Висновок       до чого приходить автор
5. Оцінка         моя думка, кому корисно

Пишуть у Present Simple: The author explains ... Вихідні дані - It was published in ...

Готові фрази: - The article is called ... It was written by ... - It was published in ... (magazine / website) in ... (year). - The article deals with ... / is devoted to ... - Стаття про ... / присвячена ... - The main idea of the article is that ... - First, the author explains ... Then the author describes ... - The author points out that ... - Автор звертає увагу на те, що ... - The author pays special attention to ... - According to the author, ... - На думку автора, ... - The author comes to the conclusion that ... - Автор приходить до висновку, що ... - In my opinion, the article is useful because ... I would recommend it to ...

Зразок (стаття вигадана, щоб показати шаблон; для здачі бери справжню):

The article is called "Quantum Computers: A New Way to Compute". It was published in a popular science magazine in [year], and the author is [name]. The article deals with quantum computers and how they are different from ordinary computers. First, the author explains that an ordinary computer works with bits, and every bit is 0 or 1. A quantum computer works with qubits. A qubit can be in a superposition of 0 and 1 at the same time. Then the author describes entanglement, a special connection between qubits. The author points out that quantum computers are very small and make a lot of errors, so scientists are working on error correction. The author comes to the conclusion that quantum computers will not replace laptops, but they will help chemistry, medicine and cryptography. In my opinion, the article is interesting and clear. I would recommend it to first-year students.

Тема 5. Усна тема: план на 10 речень

Чи буде усна тема на заліку - невідомо. Діалог про університет задавали 25.09 («5-6 фраз, але так, щоб розкрити тему»). План:

About myself       ім'я, курс, факультет, спеціальність,
                   чому фізика, предмети цього семестру
                   (Continuous!), звичайний день (Simple),
                   хобі, плани (would like to ...)
My university      назва, коли заснований (was founded),
                   факультет з 1953, ступені Bachelor /
                   Master of Science, PhD, лабораторії
My future          квантовий комп'ютер, біт і кубіт,
profession         superposition, entanglement, де
                   допоможе, що треба знати, англійська
  • I study quantum computers and quantum programming at the Faculty of Physics.
  • An ordinary computer works with bits. A quantum computer works with qubits.
  • A qubit can be in a superposition of 0 and 1 at the same time.
  • After university I would like to work in a research laboratory.
